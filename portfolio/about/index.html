My Rotaract journey began after I joined Gandaki University. At that time, I was looking for opportunities beyond academics where I could meet new people, learn new things, and develop myself through real experiences.

I came to know about the Rotaract Club of Gandaki University through my friend Prabhab Tiwari. His involvement and journey in Rotaract inspired me to become a part of the club. His experience showed me that Rotaract was not only about being a member of a club, but also about learning, serving, connecting, leading, and growing together.

With that motivation, I joined the Rotaract Club of Gandaki University as a member. My journey started with learning and participating in different club activities. Through these experiences, I got the opportunity to meet new people, work with different teams, communicate with others, and understand the value of teamwork and leadership.

As I became more involved in the club, I received a new opportunity from our Club President, Prabhab Tiwari, to take responsibility as the RTR Sports Coordinator.

Becoming the RTR Sports Coordinator was a meaningful milestone in my Rotaract journey. This role gave me the opportunity to take responsibility for sports event planning, coordination, teamwork, communication, leadership, and event management. It also challenged me to step outside my comfort zone and become more confident in taking responsibility.

What started as simply joining the club as a member gradually became an opportunity to take on a leadership role.

I am thankful to Prabhab Tiwari for inspiring me through his journey and for giving me the opportunity to contribute as the RTR Sports Coordinator.

For me, Rotaract has become a place where I can learn, connect, contribute, lead, and grow. This is not the end of my Rotaract journey—it is only the beginning of many more experiences, challenges, and opportunities ahead.

From a member to a Sports Coordinator — every step has been a part of my growth.

My journey into the world of technology began when I was in Grade 4, when my Thulobaba gifted me my first laptop. At that time, I was simply curious about computers. I wanted to explore the device, learn how different applications worked, and discover what I could do with it.

That simple gift gradually became the foundation of my learning journey. I started exploring different computer applications such as Microsoft Word, Excel, PowerPoint, and Adobe Photoshop. Every new thing I learned increased my curiosity and motivated me to spend more time understanding computers and digital technology.

What started as simple curiosity slowly developed into a genuine passion for technology.

When I reached Grade 7, I had the opportunity to choose my subjects, and I decided to study Computer Science. From that point, my interest in technology became more structured and focused.

I continued studying Computer Science throughout my school years and completed my Grade 10 education with Computer Science. During this period, I began to understand that computers were not only devices for entertainment or accessing information. They could also be used to solve problems, create new ideas, develop software, and build useful solutions.

My curiosity continued to grow as I learned more. My father played an important role in guiding and inspiring me during this stage. Through his support and encouragement, I started learning coding, software-related skills, and other fascinating areas of technology.

By the age of eleven, I had already developed practical knowledge of various computer skills. At the same time, I realized something important: technology is a field where learning never truly ends.

That realization encouraged me to continuously explore, practice, and improve myself.

My learning journey was not always easy. Like many students interested in computers and technology, I also faced difficulties during my early learning years. I worked with a low-end computer that often struggled to run programming software and other advanced applications.

Sometimes, the limitations of my computer made learning and experimenting difficult. However, I never allowed those limitations to stop me. I spent countless hours experimenting, solving problems, learning from mistakes, and improving my skills with whatever resources I had available. These experiences taught me some of the most important lessons of my life: patience, consistency, problem-solving, and the importance of never giving up.

There were also times when my family questioned why I was spending so much time on the computer. They were concerned that I was spending too much time with technology. However, I always believed that the time I invested in learning technology would eventually help me build a meaningful future.

Gradually, as my family saw my dedication, growth, and achievements, their concerns turned into encouragement and pride. Today, my family fully supports my passion and feels proud of the journey I have built through hard work and determination.

After completing Grade 10, I joined Prativa Secondary School for my +2 studies. Although I chose the Management stream, I continued my interest in technology by choosing Computer Science as one of my subjects.

This combination gave me an opportunity to understand both business and technology. I learned that technology and management can work together to create better solutions and opportunities. During my +2 years, my interest in the IT sector continued to grow, and I became increasingly determined to build my future in technology.

After completing +2, I decided to pursue higher education in the Information Technology sector. I started exploring different colleges in Pokhara and researching the available IT programs. I wanted an environment where I could not only study theory but also gain practical knowledge, technical skills, project experience, and exposure to innovation.

During this search, I discovered Gandaki University. The university attracted me because of its focus on technology, practical learning, innovation, and student development. Its learning environment, including computer labs, electronic labs, library resources, technology-enabled classrooms, project-based learning, and other student facilities, gave me confidence that it could provide the environment I was looking for.

I decided to pursue a Bachelor of Information Technology (BIT) at Gandaki University.

Joining Gandaki University marked another important chapter in my journey. The journey that started with a laptop in Grade 4 had now developed into a clear academic and career goal in the field of Information Technology.

At university, I continued strengthening my technical knowledge and practical skills. I explored areas such as programming, web development, databases, software development, networking, AI/ML, and modern digital technologies.

University life also taught me that technology is not only about writing code or using computers. It is about understanding problems, creating solutions, working with people, communicating ideas, and continuously adapting to new technologies.

I began working on practical projects and experimenting with different technologies. Each project gave me a new opportunity to learn something, make mistakes, solve problems, and improve myself.

My university journey also provided me with opportunities beyond academics. As I became more involved in university activities, I developed an interest in leadership, teamwork, communication, and community service. My involvement in the Rotaract Club of Gandaki University gave me opportunities to work with different people, organize activities, contribute to the community, and develop leadership skills.

“Insight to Impact.”

For me, leadership is another form of learning. It is about turning ideas into action, working together, supporting others, and creating meaningful impact. The values of service, leadership, fellowship, innovation, and technology have become important parts of my personal journey.

When I look back at my journey, I can see how every stage has contributed to where I am today. First Laptop → Curiosity → Computer Applications → Computer Science → Coding → Challenges → +2 with Computer Science → Interest in IT → Gandaki University → BIT → Practical Projects → Leadership → Future in Technology

The laptop my Thulobabba gave me in Grade 4 introduced me to the world of technology. Choosing Computer Science in Grade 7 gave my curiosity direction. My +2 studies helped me understand both management and technology. Joining Gandaki University transformed that interest into a more focused academic and professional journey.

My challenges taught me patience and determination. My family taught me the importance of support and encouragement. My projects taught me problem-solving and practical skills. And my involvement in Rotaract has taught me leadership, teamwork, service, and responsibility.

Today, I continue to strengthen my knowledge and skills in Information Technology. I am exploring programming, web development, databases, software development, networking, AI/ML, and other emerging technologies.

I know that the technology industry is constantly changing. New technologies appear every day, and the skills that are important today may continue to evolve tomorrow. Therefore, I believe that the most important skill is not simply knowing one technology. It is having the curiosity and willingness to keep learning.

My journey is still continuing. There will be new challenges, new technologies, new opportunities, and new lessons ahead. I want to continue learning, building practical solutions, helping others, and using technology to create meaningful impact.
